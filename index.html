R-012026

MedTokenBudget: Lesion-Preserving Token Routing for Dermoscopic Classification

Sole author · arXiv preprint

Keeping lesion evidence while using fewer image tokens.

Details +

Lesion-supervised token routing after a frozen vision backbone. At a 30% token budget on ISIC 2019, 80.38 ± 0.18% accuracy versus 76.93 ± 0.03% for ToMe across three seeds; the backbone is unchanged.

arXiv:2609.30613 ↗

↗
R-022026

Mass-Conservation Token Transformers for Auditable MS/MS Spectrum Retrieval

Submitted to AAAI 2027

Retrieving spectra with evidence grounded in mass conservation.

Details +

Mass-conserving fragment pairs as sparse evidence tokens, with mass- and overlap-based hard negatives. Evidence-removal experiments test whether the exported evidence affects ranking.

↗
R-032025

Efficient Attention via Pre-Scoring

First author · Under review

Selecting informative key intervals before computing attention.

Details +

Advised by David P. Woodruff (CMU). Pre-scoring and informative-key selection implemented in Triton; integrated with HyperAttention, it reduced ChatGLM perplexity from 12.0 to 9.5 at 131k-token contexts under a fixed interaction budget. Not a measured speedup.

arXiv:2505.11040 ↗

↗
R-042026

Machine Learning for Antiviral Drug Discovery

USC · Yan Liu group · Spring 2026

Reproducible molecular benchmarks and model interpretation.

Details +

Benchmarked descriptor-based models, Chemprop, and hybrid approaches across seven Therapeutics Data Commons datasets using scaffold splits; descriptor importance and SHAP attributions examined features associated with predicted antiviral activity.
