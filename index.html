Paper · 2026

MedTokenBudget

Keeping lesion evidence while using fewer image tokens.

Lesion-supervised token routing after a frozen vision backbone. At a 30% token budget on ISIC 2019: 80.38 ± 0.18% versus 76.93 ± 0.03% for ToMe across three seeds. The backbone is unchanged.

The router is trained with mask supervision, a budget curriculum, diversity regularization, and attention distillation; a separate audit asks how much ground-truth lesion evidence remains after routing.

Sole author · arXiv preprint

Paper · 2025

Efficient Attention via Pre-Scoring

Selecting informative key intervals before approximate attention.

Implemented in Triton and integrated with HyperAttention. At 131k-token contexts under a fixed interaction budget, ChatGLM perplexity fell from 12.0 to 9.5. This is not a measured speedup.

Compared with HyperAttention and LevAttention on long-context language modeling.

First author · under review
