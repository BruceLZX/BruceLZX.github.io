01Paper · 2026

MedTokenBudget

A lesion-aware router that keeps diagnostic evidence when a frozen backbone is forced to read fewer tokens.

Approach

Medical images spend most of their tokens on background. I train a lightweight router after a frozen vision backbone to keep lesion evidence under a fixed token budget, using mask supervision, a budget curriculum, and attention distillation.

Evidence

On ISIC 2019 at a 30% budget, three seeds average 80.38 ± 0.18% versus 76.93 ± 0.03% for ToMe. The backbone is untouched, so the gain comes from what the model is allowed to see.

02Paper · 2025

Efficient Attention via Pre-Scoring

A cheap pre-scoring pass that picks the key intervals worth attending to at 131k tokens.

Method

Approximate attention usually decides which blocks matter after the fact. I score key intervals before attention, then spend a fixed interaction budget only on the intervals that carry signal — implemented in Triton and integrated with HyperAttention.

Result

At 131k-token contexts, ChatGLM perplexity falls from 12.0 to 9.5 against HyperAttention and LevAttention. This is a quality result, not a measured wall-clock speedup.
