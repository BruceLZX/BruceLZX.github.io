Publications & Manuscripts

MedTokenBudget: Lesion-Preserving Token Routing for Dermoscopic Image Classification

2026

Zhexiang Li (sole author). Preprint · arXiv:2609.30613

Developed lesion-supervised post-backbone token routing. At a 30% token budget on ISIC 2019, achieved 80.38 ± 0.18% accuracy versus 76.93 ± 0.03% for ToMe across three seeds; the frozen backbone remains unchanged.

Mass-Conservation Token Transformers for Auditable MS/MS Spectrum Retrieval

2026

Manuscript submitted to AAAI 2027

Represented mass-conserving fragment pairs as sparse evidence tokens for spectrum retrieval. Used closest-mass and overlap-hard negatives, alongside evidence-removal tests, to examine whether the exported evidence affects ranking.

Efficient Attention via Pre-Scoring: Prioritizing Informative Keys in Transformers

First-author work advised by Prof. David P. Woodruff (CMU). Proposed pre-scoring and implemented informative-key selection in Triton. Integrated with HyperAttention, reduced ChatGLM perplexity from 12.0 to 9.5 at 131k-token contexts under a fixed interaction budget.
